# Changelog

## 0.1.0 – unreleased

First development version (Batch 1 of `docs/PLAN.md`).

- Cart capture from the block checkout (Store API), classic checkout, a checkout listener script and logged-in customers.
- Three-step default sequence and a two-step calm sequence, quiet hours, cooldowns.
- Personal coupons with exact expiry and automatic deletion.
- One-click restore links and unsubscribe (including RFC 8058 one-click POST).
- E-mail renderer with brand tokens, store packs and English, Slovenian and German copy with placeholder fallbacks.
- "Payment failed" reminder for unpaid online orders.
- Admin overview, settings, preview and test e-mails.
- Privacy exporter, eraser, retention and policy text.
